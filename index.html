Turf Rush Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Turf Rush is an arena game for Android, published by Md. Mosharaf Hossain. The game itself works offline. This policy explains what happens to information when you play it, including the ads it shows.

Information we collect

We do not ask for an account and we do not collect your name, email or contacts. We do not run our own servers for this game.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Turf Rush shows ads from Google AdMob. Most are optional rewarded videos you choose to watch for a free revive, double crystals or an extra spin. An occasional full-screen ad may appear between games. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • How you interact with ads, and basic device, app and crash information

Google uses this to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Ads may be personalised where the law allows. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings under Google > Ads. Learn more in Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Information stored on your device

To keep your progress, the game saves the following on your phone only: the player name you type, crystals, level, items you own or equip, game statistics, quest and daily reward progress, and your settings. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. You can also erase it at any time with Settings > Reset progress inside the game.

Permissions

The app uses the internet and the advertising ID permission for ads, and vibration for game feedback (you can switch vibration off in Settings). It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, GPS location or files.

Children

Turf Rush is meant for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience with parental guidance.

Changes

If a future version changes how information is handled, for example by adding online play, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
