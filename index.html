Turf Rush Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Turf Rush is an offline arena game for Android, published by Md. Mosharaf Hossain. This policy explains what happens to information when you play it.

Information we collect

None. Turf Rush does not ask for an account, does not collect personal information, and does not send any data to us or to third parties. The game has no ads, analytics or tracking in this version.

Information stored on your device

To keep your progress, the game saves the following on your phone only: the player name you type, crystals, level, items you own or equip, game statistics, quest and daily reward progress, and your settings. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. You can also erase it at any time with Settings > Reset progress inside the game.

Permissions

The app uses vibration for game feedback, which you can switch off in Settings. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

Children

Turf Rush does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data, such as ads or online play, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
